ABOUT OUR MADRASAH

A place to learn the Qur’an with purpose, care and consistency.

Al-Qutūfud Daaniyah Online Qur’anic Academy is built around a simple purpose: to help learners develop a lasting relationship with the Book of Allah and gain sound Islamic knowledge in a supportive learning environment.

Our online madrasah brings Qur’an recitation, Tajweed, memorisation and essential Islamic Studies closer to students who may not have convenient access to physical classes. Lessons can be followed from home through a laptop or phone, while students receive guidance, correction and encouragement from their tutors.

We value discipline, sincerity, good character and steady progress. Whether a learner is beginning from the basics, improving recitation, memorising the Qur’an or seeking structured Islamic learning, our aim is to make every lesson beneficial and purposeful.
