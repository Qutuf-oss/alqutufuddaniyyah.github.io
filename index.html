ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Learning that is personal, organised and purposeful.

Al-Qutūfud Daaniyah Online Quranic Academy is designed to make Qur’anic and Islamic learning accessible to learners around the world through convenient online classes.

Our approach combines consistent lessons, revision, practical correction and mentorship so learners can make steady progress.

01Assessment
We identify the learner’s current level and learning needs.
02Personalised plan
A suitable programme and timetable are agreed with the learner/parent.
03Regular lessons
Lessons are delivered online with clear goals and revision.
04Progress monitoring
Feedback helps learners know what to improve next.
